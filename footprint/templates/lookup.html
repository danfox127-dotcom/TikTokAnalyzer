What this looked up, and what it won't
  • GravatarAsked with a scrambled code made from the address, the way Gravatar lookups always work.
  • GitHubSearched for public commits made with the address.
  • The address's domainAsked whether it points to a fediverse profile (never big webmail providers), and its public mail records were read — only the domain name is sent for those.
  • Not doneTrying sign-up or password-reset pages to see whether the address has an account somewhere: that misuses those systems and can send the owner an alert.
  • Your own addressTo see whether it's in known data breaches, type it into Have I Been Pwned yourself.

Why there's no "who owns this number". A number's facts come from how phone numbers are laid out, so nothing is sent anywhere. The area is where the number's prefix was first assigned, not where anyone is, and numbers move between carriers. Services that name a number's owner are data brokers, and checking messaging apps for a number exposes the owner — Footprint does neither.

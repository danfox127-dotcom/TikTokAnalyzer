What you can change, in the TikTok app

  1. Stop personalised ads. Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Ads → turn off Personalised ads.
  2. Stop other apps reporting back. On the same Ads page, turn off Activity from ad partners and clear it.
  3. Clear what you watched. Settings and privacy → Activity centre → Watch history → Clear.
  4. Ask for it again, or delete it. Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data (or Deactivate or delete account).

TikTok renames these now and then; the words above are close to what you'll see.

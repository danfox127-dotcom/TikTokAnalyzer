🔄 Keep it in sync

New saves find their own way in

The platforms won't hand your saves to another app, so Faves does the next best thing: it picks up every export the moment you download it, syncs your YouTube playlists by itself, and reminds you when an export is due.

One-time setup (about 10 minutes)
  1. At Google Cloud, create a project (any name, e.g. “Faves”).
  2. In APIs & Services → Library, switch on YouTube Data API v3.
  3. In OAuth consent screen: choose External, fill in the app name and your email, add yourself as a user, then press Publish app (otherwise Google signs you out every 7 days).
  4. In Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID, choose Desktop app.
  5. Copy its client ID and secret into ~/.favorites.env:
    GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID="…"
    GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET="…"
  6. Close the Faves window and double-click start.command again. A Connect button appears here.

🇪🇺 TikTok's sharing tool

Automatic TikTok sync works in Europe and the UK only

TikTok's Data Portability API lets you send your favourites straight to an app you choose — but it only returns data for accounts in the EEA and UK, and apps have to pass TikTok's review. For everyone else the export above is the way. We'll look again if that changes.

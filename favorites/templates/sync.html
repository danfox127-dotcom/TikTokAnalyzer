🔄 Keep it in sync

New saves find their own way in

The platforms won't hand your saves to another app, so Faves does the next best thing: it picks up every export the moment you download it, syncs your YouTube playlists by itself, and reminds you when an export is due.

  1. At Google Cloud, create a project (any name, e.g. “Faves”).

💻 Your computer

Keep any page in Chrome, in one click

The Faves extension works in Chrome, Edge, Arc and Brave. Click its icon to keep the page you're on, right-click a link to keep that, or press ⌘⇧K.

{{ icon('plus') }}Download the Chrome extension

  1. Open the download to unzip it, and move the faves-extension folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. Chrome keeps using it from there.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the faves-extension folder. Then pin Faves from the puzzle-piece icon.
  4. In the settings page that opens, enter the address above without /save{% if anywhere or at_home %} ({{ anywhere or at_home }}){% endif %} and just the password, without “Bearer”. Click Save and test, then Allow.

🇪🇺 TikTok's sharing tool

Automatic TikTok sync works in Europe and the UK only

TikTok's Data Portability API lets you send your favourites straight to an app you choose — but it only returns data for accounts in the EEA and UK, and apps have to pass TikTok's review. For everyone else the export above is the way. We'll look again if that changes.
